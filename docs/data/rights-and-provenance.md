# Rights & Provenance Rules

1. Public availability on a website does not automatically grant redistribution rights.
2. Ancient canonical text and modern editions/translations must be tracked separately.
3. Every imported record should carry source, edition, URL, license, provenance, import date, and version/checksum when available.
4. Do not silently normalize away source text. Keep raw/original and normalized representations when transformations are applied.
5. Human review is required before a record is promoted to a scholarly/verified status.
6. A parser or AI suggestion is never automatically equivalent to a human-verified grammatical or textual analysis.
7. Research-related cross-links must state whether they are navigational, textual, lexical, commentary, or scholarly relations.
